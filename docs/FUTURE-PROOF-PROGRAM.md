# Future Proof — Program Design

**Status:** Draft v1 (September 2026). Pages: `future-proof.html`, `future-proof-apply.html`, `future-proof-one-pager.html`.

## The one-line pitch

A free eight-week, remote program for people aged 16–24 that teaches them to work with AI by building something real for their community, and ends with a published project, a verifiable credential, and a Demo Day in front of employers.

## Why this program, why FinMango

- **The problem is the first rung.** AI absorbs the routine, well-defined tasks that junior roles are made of. Early evidence (Stanford Digital Economy Lab, Aug 2025) shows a ~13% relative employment decline for 22–25 year olds in the most AI-exposed occupations since late 2022. The IMF (Jan 2024) puts ~40% of global employment as AI-exposed (~60% in advanced economies).
- **"Learn AI" is not actionable.** Young people are told to learn AI without being told what that means or how to prove it to a hiring manager. Certificates about AI are already commoditised. A shipped project built with AI, published publicly, presented to employers, is not.
- **FinMango's angle is money.** Job displacement, income volatility, reskilling costs and a thin safety net are financial health problems. That framing is distinctive, matches our mission, and is what our research (AI Economic Signal, Barometer) already measures.
- **We already have the parts.** Classroom delivery at scale (Millionaire Mindset, 30+ schools/yr, 100K+ students), a global youth network (Ambassadors, 15+ countries), a competition format with employer judges (Barrier Breakers), a research product to teach from (AI Economic Signal), and an open-by-default publishing habit (Ambassador Notes, published reviews). Future Proof is the connective tissue, not a sixth silo.

## Design principles

1. **Project first.** The unit of value is a shipped, public project. Everything else (credential, Demo Day, network) hangs off it.
2. **Human skills through the work, not lectures.** Each week has one deliverable and one human skill it teaches implicitly (judgment, listening, writing, presenting).
3. **Honest about AI.** No fear-mongering, no hype. Week one is numbers. Week three is "AI as a colleague, not an oracle": verify, edit, catch it when it's wrong.
4. **Free, always.** No tuition, no paid tier, no charge for the credential. Free tiers of mainstream AI tools only.
5. **Open by default.** Projects, curriculum, assessments and follow-up results are published.
6. **Cohort, not course.** Fixed dates, teams of three, weekly live session. Completion rates in cohort models are dramatically higher than self-paced MOOCs.

## Audience

- Ages 16–24, any country, English-language delivery. Projects may serve communities in any language.
- Two entry points:
  - **Open cohort** (the core program): apply individually, remote, 8 weeks.
  - **Classroom edition**: 5-day in-school version taught alongside Millionaire Mindset; a funnel into the open cohort.
- No prerequisites. No coding required.

## Curriculum

| Week | Phase | Deliverable | Human skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read | One-page brief: what the AI Economic Signal says about my region/field | Reading data without hype |
| 2 | Read | Personal runway plan: irregular income, reskilling cost, emergency fund | Financial planning under uncertainty |
| 3 | Build | Team formed, barrier chosen, AI verification exercise completed | Judgment |
| 4 | Build | Five user interviews written up | Listening / interviewing |
| 5 | Build | Version one of the project | Making |
| 6 | Build | Revised version after user testing | Taking feedback |
| 7 | Ship | Project published on finmango.org + written build story | Writing for others |
| 8 | Ship | Demo Day presentation (5 min + 5 min Q&A) | Presenting |

**Project shapes:** tool/calculator, plain-language guide, data story, campaign, open. Each must address one financial barrier faced by real people the team can reach.

**Weekly rhythm (~5 hrs):** 60–75 min live session (recorded), 45–60 min team meeting, 2.5–3 hrs build time. Weekly mentor check-in from week 3.

## What participants leave with

1. A published project on finmango.org (permanent URL, open licence, names attached) plus a build story.
2. A FinMango Future Proof credential, issued only on shipping, linking to the project (self-verifying). Consider Open Badges / verifiable credential format.
3. Demo Day in front of employer judges, with written feedback from every judge; prizes for top teams.
4. Introductions to employer partners; priority consideration for Global Ambassadors and volunteer roles.

## Delivery model

- **Cohort size:** 60–90 participants (20–30 teams) per cohort to start; two cohorts per year (Spring, Fall).
- **Staffing per cohort:** 1 program lead (0.5 FTE), 1 curriculum/teaching lead (Bob-style instructor, contract), 8–12 volunteer mentors (employer partners, alumni ambassadors), Demo Day judge panel of 6–10.
- **Platform:** existing stack. Live sessions on Zoom/Meet; team channels on Discord or Slack; project pages published via the same Cloudflare Pages + Apps Script pattern as Ambassador Notes; applications via Apps Script → Google Sheet (same as Barrier Breakers apply form).
- **Classroom edition:** 5 × 50-minute sessions, one project per class, taught by FinMango instructors alongside Millionaire Mindset. Teacher one-pager, slides, rubric.

## Measurement (all published)

- **Skills:** pre/post assessment covering AI fluency (including verification), financial planning, data reading. The AI-Ready assessment can be adapted as the instrument.
- **Output:** % of enrolled who ship; % of projects with documented real-world use.
- **Outcomes:** 6-month follow-up: employment / education status, earnings change, whether the project appeared in an application or interview.
- **Voice:** every participant review published unedited (as with the 16,000+ Millionaire Mindset reviews).
- **Equity:** geographic and income distribution of participants vs applicants.

## Alignment with workforce funders

Most corporate-foundation youth-employment calls (e.g. Citi Foundation Global Innovation Challenge 2026) ask for three things. Future Proof maps directly:

| Funder ask | Future Proof |
|---|---|
| Training in AI and human-centric skills | Weeks 1–8; one human skill per week; AI verification as core content |
| Expanded access to digital tools | Free program, free-tier tools, remote, any country; classroom edition reaches students without home access |
| Connection to employment opportunities | Employer mentors, Demo Day, written feedback, introductions, credential |

Plus what funders rarely get: public, auditable outcomes.

## Risks and mitigations

- **Completion.** Mitigate with cohort model, teams, fixed dates, weekly mentor contact, and a small, visible deliverable every week.
- **AI tool access / cost.** Free tiers only; curriculum is tool-agnostic; classroom edition for those without home access.
- **Quality of published work.** Editorial pass before publishing (same as Ambassador Notes); rubric shared from week 3.
- **Employer engagement fades.** Ask for small, bounded commitments (1 hr/week for 6 weeks, or one Demo Day); rotate partners across cohorts.
- **Brand sprawl.** Future Proof sits across existing programs (Signal → teaching content, Barrier Breakers → Demo Day format, Ambassadors → alumni path). Retire or fold the unlinked `ai-ready/` and `talent/` pages rather than running them in parallel.
