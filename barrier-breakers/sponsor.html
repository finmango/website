Where the money goes

The prizes and the day come to about $25,000 for 250 students, roughly $100 a seat. On top of that, we pay a student to run the program.

  • $17,500 · Prize poolThe floor for cash to the five Demo Day teams and five honorable mentions. 2026 raised more, so awards topped $20,000.
  • $5,000 · The dayBreakfast, lunch, and materials for roughly 300 students, teachers, and judges.
  • $2,500 · Running itVenue, AV, printing, and insurance for an event this size.
  • Staff · Student program managerA paid student who recruits the schools, judges, and sponsors.

Raise more than that, and every extra dollar goes into the prize pool. No new line items, just bigger checks for the students. In 2026 we raised $30,000, and every dollar over budget went to the students: more than $20,000 in awards to the winning teams and their schools. The 250 students came from more than 25 high schools across the Akron, Canton, and Cleveland areas.
