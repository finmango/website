What happens with your words

  • They go where decisions get made. Grant applications, reports to funders and partners, and our website.
  • You choose the credit. Your name and school, your school only, or anonymous.
  • We keep your meaning. We may trim for length. We won't change what you said.
  • You can change your mind. Email us any time and we'll edit it or take it down.
