The problem

Financial health isn't about knowing more. It's about facing fewer walls.

The barriers are real and specific: transportation that costs more than the job pays, rent that eats 60% of a paycheck, medical debt from a single ER visit. These are problems that take new ideas, not just tighter budgets.

How it works

Three steps. No fluff.

01

Pick a barrier

Choose a track: transportation, housing, healthcare, financial literacy, or bring your own. Research why it exists and who it hurts most.

02

Build a solution

App prototype, community tool, financial literacy campaign, policy proposal. Use AI tools. Prototype fast. Make it real.

03

Present and compete

Demo Day for oral presentations. Innovation Gallery for posters. Knowledge Track for financial literacy. Everyone who applies competes.
