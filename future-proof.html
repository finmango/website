Learn to work with AI. Then prove it.

A free eight-week program for people 16 to 24. Understand what AI is really doing to work, build something real for your community with it, and leave with a published project, a credential, and a room of employers who watched you present.

Why now

The first job was the on-ramp. AI is taking the on-ramp.

For a century the deal was simple: get a junior job, learn on someone else's payroll, work your way up. That first rung is exactly where AI lands hardest, because it is made of the routine, well-defined tasks a new hire used to learn on. Young people are being told to "learn AI" without anyone saying what that means, or how to prove it to the person hiring.

Future Proof is our answer. Not a certificate about AI. A real thing you built with it, published under your name, in front of people who hire.

We designed it to run lean, because that's what we can afford today: recorded lessons, teams that review each other's work, and a stipend that pays people to finish. Funding adds the human part.

How it works

Three phases. One shipped project.

About five hours a week, remote, on your own schedule, in a cohort of people your age from around the world. Every week has a deadline and a deliverable. Everything you make is yours, and everything you make gets published.

Weeks 1–2 · Read
Read the economy
Two short recorded briefs and two written tasks. What AI is and isn't doing to jobs, in numbers instead of headlines. Map your own field with FinMango's AI Economic Signal and write a money plan for a career that will not be linear.
Weeks 3–6 · Build
Build with AI
Teams of three pick a financial barrier in their own community and build a real response: a tool, a guide, a data story, a campaign. Each week you review two other teams' work against a rubric, and they review yours. AI does the grunt work. You do the thinking and the checking.
Weeks 7–8 · Ship
Ship and show
Publish the project on finmango.org with your name on it. Submit a five-minute Demo Day video that employer judges score and write feedback on. Collect your stipend, your credential, and introductions.
The eight weeks

Every week ends with something you made.

No lectures about the future of work. Each week is a short recorded brief, one job to finish by Sunday, and one human skill it quietly teaches, because the skills that hold their value are the ones a model cannot do for you.

Presenting
What you build

Pick the shape of your project.

Every project answers one financial barrier that real people in your community face. The form it takes is up to your team.

A tool or calculator
A paycheck estimator for gig workers in your city. A "can I afford this apartment" check for first renters. Small, useful, and yours.
A plain-language guide
The explainer you wish existed: student loans in your country, your first tax return, what a benefits letter actually says.
A data story
Take a public dataset, or FinMango's own, and show something true about your town that nobody has put in one chart yet.
A campaign
Get a real audience to do a real thing: open a first account, apply for aid they qualify for, show up to a workshop you run.
Something we haven't thought of
If it removes a financial barrier for someone and you can ship it in six weeks, it counts.
What you leave with
A portfolio, not a promise.
Most programs end with a PDF. This one ends with money in your account and four things a hiring manager can check.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Do I need to know how to code?
No. Some teams build software, most don't. A guide, a data story, or a campaign is just as valid a project as an app. What we teach is how to use AI tools well and how to check their work, and that applies whether or not you ever write a line of code.
I've never used AI tools. Is that a problem?
It's the point. Week three starts from zero. If you've used them a lot already, you'll spend that week learning to catch the mistakes you've been letting through.
Is it really free?
Yes. No application fee, no tuition, no paid tier, no charge for the credential. FinMango has never charged a student for anything since 2017 and this program is no different. We use the free tiers of mainstream AI tools, so you won't be asked to buy a subscription either.
How is this different from an online course?
A course ends with a certificate that says you watched videos. Future Proof ends with a thing you built, published on a real website, that you presented to real employers, plus written feedback from them. You also do it in a cohort with a team, on a schedule, which is why people actually finish.
What if I'm not in the United States?
Apply. FinMango's ambassadors come from 15+ countries and we expect the founding cohort to be global. Live sessions are recorded, and we'll cluster teams by time zone where we can. Your project should serve your own community, wherever that is.
How are people selected?
We read every application. We're looking for a problem you actually care about and evidence you'll finish what you start, not grades or prior experience. If the cohort fills, everyone who applied is first in line for the next one.
When does it start?
When the founding cohort is funded. We're raising for it now. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to hear the dates, and the size of the waitlist is part of how we make the case to funders, so signing up helps.
Who runs it week to week?
In the lean version: the recorded briefs, your team, the two teams reviewing you, and a FinMango ambassador who hosts your group and answers questions. One FinMango staff member approves projects before they publish and reads the inbox. With funding, a dedicated program lead and an employer mentor per team are added. We're upfront about this because the difference is real, and it's what we're raising money for.
How does the stipend work?
You're paid at two milestones: when your team completes its five interviews in week four, and when your project is published in week seven. Nobody is paid for signing up. Amounts are set with the founding funder and published before the cohort opens. If you're under 18 we pay a guardian or use gift cards, and we pay by batch transfer to any country we can reach.
What happens to my project afterwards?
It stays published on finmango.org under an open license, with your name on it, for as long as the site exists. You keep the right to build on it, take it further, or turn it into something bigger. Several will end up used by real people, which is the whole idea.
