The Story

At a pre-college summer program at Rice, the conversation eventually turned to the $1,500 stipend each of us was about to receive.

Most of us had never held that much money at once.

Most of us were first-generation or low-income. Only a few had ever saved or managed money.

The Gap

Research stipends worth thousands. Work-study. Part-time jobs. And still, many students feel unprepared to manage any of it.

  • Concerning.
  • Unpredictable.
  • Unsustainable.

Why shouldn’t there be preparation for handling this kind of privilege?

Before a new journey, main characters get tools for what’s ahead. Incoming students deserve the same. Workshops, or at the very least structured lectures.
