## Alt text (paste per slide on Instagram, or into the PDF post's description)

1. A portrait of David Johnson, smiling, above the headline "Preparation before
   privilege."
2. "At a pre-college summer program at Rice, the conversation eventually turned
   to the $1,500 stipend each of us was about to receive. Most of us had never
   held that much money at once. Most of us were first-generation or
   low-income. Only a few had ever saved or managed money."
3. "Research stipends worth thousands. Work-study. Part-time jobs. And still,
   many students feel unprepared to manage any of it." Then three words, one
   per line: Concerning. Unpredictable. Unsustainable.
4. On black, under the words "Without a foundation, the cycle is": Earn. Spend.
   Wait for the next stipend. Below: "And it is dangerously easy to carry into
   adult life after graduation."
5. Two definitions. High school: room for questionable spending, with a
   dependable line of support and advice behind you. College: the support is
   still there, but less active and less immediate, and college is not simply
   an extension of high school.
6. On black: "More money is not the solution. The student becomes a consumer
   rather than a strategy manager." Attributed to David Johnson, FinMango
   Ambassador.
7. "Why shouldn't there be preparation for handling this kind of privilege?
   Before a new journey, main characters get tools for what's ahead. Incoming
   students deserve the same. Workshops, or at the very least structured
   lectures."
8. Closing slide. "Not just to succeed in class. To start building their own
   inheritance." A portrait of David Johnson, FinMango Ambassador, Rice
   University, and where to read the full note.
