## LinkedIn — document post (`finmango-foundation-for-peace-linkedin.pdf`)

"When survival consumes every decision, planning for tomorrow becomes a
privilege rather than a possibility."

María Manuela Córdoba Aguirre is a FinMango Ambassador and a human rights
lawyer working with victims of armed conflict in Colombia. She writes that for
people who have lived through displacement or the destruction of a livelihood,
financial health starts somewhere most of us take for granted: the opportunity
to start again.

Her argument runs from there to peace. Give people the tools to regain control
of their own lives and they can take part in their communities, support their
families, and invest in a future. Invest in financial health and you are not
only improving economic outcomes. You are laying a foundation for lasting
peace.

## Posting notes

- **Instagram**: upload all eight PNGs in order. They are 1440x1800 (4:5),
  which is Instagram's own ceiling, so nothing gets upscaled. Put the post
  link in the bio or in Stories, since the caption can't carry one.
- **LinkedIn**: post the PDF as a document, not an image. LinkedIn renders it
  as a swipeable carousel and keeps the type sharp. Give the upload a title:
  "Financial Health Is a Foundation for Peace." Page 8 shows the URL, and the
  caption carries the clickable link.
- Tag María and let her post her own version too. A first-person share from
  the author outruns the org account on both platforms.
- Slide 5 is the strongest single image if you want a non-carousel post.
- Check the cover photo before it goes out. It shows identifiable members of a
  conflict-affected community, and consent for a workshop photo on the note's
  own page is not the same as consent to be the lead image of a campaign post.
  If nobody can confirm it, slide 5 or slide 7 works as an alternate opener.
