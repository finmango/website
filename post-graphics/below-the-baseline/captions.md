## A note on the figures

Slides 2 and 3 put four numbers in display type: 54.1%, 75%, 90%, and 4%.
All four come from Hilya's note, and the slides credit it by name. But the
note itself does not say where the numbers came from, and a percentage set
at 250px on a FinMango canvas reads as a FinMango data claim rather than an
ambassador's citation.

Before these go out, someone should pin each figure to its origin — Namibia
Statistics Agency, FinScope Namibia, or the City of Windhoek tariff schedule
for the 4% — and either add that source to the slide's credit line or drop
the figure. The note's own currency is worth checking too: Namibian income
statistics are normally reported in Namibian dollars, and the note gives the
threshold in US dollars ($122 in the body, $122.74 in the dek).

If you'd rather not carry the figures at all, the deck still works without
them: slides 4 through 8 make the same argument qualitatively, and dropping
2 and 3 leaves a six-slide carousel.
