# 2026 Ambassador Spotlight Series — Captions & Schedule

Ready-to-post captions for FinMango's own channels (LinkedIn, Instagram,
X/Twitter). One ambassador per week. Pair each caption with that
ambassador's graphic: `<slug>-square.png` for the feed,
`<slug>-vertical.png` for Stories.

These are different from `captions.md` in this folder — that file is
templates for the ambassadors to announce themselves. This one is us
promoting them.

Voice notes: short, concrete, one real detail per person. No
"thrilled to announce," no em dashes, no hashtag walls. Two hashtags max.

  can reshare. Their reshare is worth more than our post.

  than posting both the same day. The order above is a suggestion, not
  a contract.

  morning. Use the vertical for an Instagram Story that links to
