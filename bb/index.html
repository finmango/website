The day

Full schedule
01

Find your market

Pick a cost that keeps families from getting ahead. Work out who it hurts, what it costs them, and why nobody has fixed it.

02

Build the venture

An app, a product, a service, a community program, a policy campaign. Decide how it reaches people and how it keeps running.

03

Pitch the panel

Two judges visit your table like investors working a floor. You pitch for 60 seconds, then take their questions.
