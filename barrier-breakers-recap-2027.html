Reach 8,000 students. Put the top 250 in one room.

Barrier Breakers is FinMango's one-day innovation challenge for Northeast Ohio high schoolers, heading into its fourth year. In 2026, powered by Valmark, we promoted it to roughly 8,000 students in 25+ high schools across the Akron, Canton, and Cleveland areas through our in-person classroom programming over the academic year. About 250 of the top students from those classrooms showed up on March 20 to pitch real solutions to the costs that hold families back. For 2027 we are asking Valmark to return as platinum sponsor at $25,000. The details are on page 3.

How the pipeline worked

In the classroom. FinMango teaches in-person financial education in 25+ high schools across the Akron, Canton, and Cleveland areas, reaching about 8,000 students over the academic year. That is where students first hear about Barrier Breakers.

The build. Teams of one to three pick a real barrier, such as transportation, housing, healthcare, or financial literacy, and build a solution to bring to the event.

Event day. Sixty-second pitches to a three-judge panel in the morning. Five finalists present to the whole room at Demo Day in the afternoon, and the cash goes to the teams.

Where the money went

Barrier Breakers 2026 raised $30,000 in total: $25,000 from Valmark as platinum sponsor and $5,000 from two additional sponsors to help cover expenses. More than $20,000 went out as awards. The rest paid for the day itself: breakfast and lunch for 250 students plus their teachers and judges, materials, and the space.

In 2026, part of each award was a scholarship to the host university rather than cash the students could take home. Scholarships will not come out of the sponsorship budget in 2027. A host university may still offer them as its own contribution, but sponsor dollars go to the prizes and the event itself.

Sponsoring Barrier Breakers 2027

We are asking Valmark to return as the platinum presenting sponsor of Barrier Breakers 2027 at $25,000, the same level as 2026. The day keeps the same rhythm, a morning of pitches at the tables and Demo Day in the afternoon, but we have simplified the format for 2027: one pipeline for every team, no tracks to declare, one rubric for both rounds, and cash straight to the student teams. With no scholarship component, more of each sponsor dollar reaches the students in the room. The invitation list grows: beyond the 25+ schools where FinMango teaches, we plan to invite many more high schools across Northeast Ohio to bring teams.

What sponsorship funds

The prize pool. Cash to the five Demo Day teams, the largest share of the $20,000+ awarded in 2026.

Honorable mentions. A prize for each section's runner-up, so ten teams leave with something.

The day. Breakfast, lunch, and materials for 250 students plus their teachers and judges.

The next cohort. Classroom visits and the teacher toolkit that get schools to bring teams in the first place.

Operations and contingency. Venue, AV, printing, insurance, staff time on the day, and a reserve for the unexpected costs of an event this size.

FinMango is a very lean nonprofit with little overhead. The full sponsorship is invested in the event: prizes first, then the operations that make the day run, with a reserve for the unexpected. No scholarships will be funded from sponsorship in 2027.
