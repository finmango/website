Barrier Breakers · Sponsor Recap for 2027

Reach 8,000 students.
Put 300+ of the best in one room.

Barrier Breakers is FinMango's one-day innovation challenge for Northeast Ohio high schoolers. In 2026, powered by Valmark, we promoted it to roughly 8,000 students through our in-person classroom programming. More than 300 of the top students from those classrooms showed up on March 20 to pitch real solutions to the costs that hold families back.

How the pipeline worked

1

Teach in the classroom

FinMango delivers in-person financial education in Northeast Ohio high schools, reaching about 8,000 students over the year.

2

Invite the standouts

Students build a solution to a real barrier: transportation, housing, healthcare, or financial literacy. Teams of one to three register.

3

Compete on event day

Sixty-second pitches to a three-judge panel in the morning. Five finalists present to the whole room at Demo Day. Cash goes to the teams.
