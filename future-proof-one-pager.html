A FinMango program · Ages 18–24 · Free

Learn to work with AI. Then prove it.

Future Proof is a free eight-week, remote program for young people. They learn what AI is really doing to work, build something real for their own community with it, and leave with a published project, a verifiable credential, and employer-judged Demo Day feedback. Not a certificate about AI. A shipped thing built with it. Designed to run lean on recorded lessons and peer review; a sponsor adds a stipend paid for finishing, then the human layer.

How it works 3 phases · 8 weeks

Weeks 1–2Read the economy

Recorded briefs and written tasks. AI and jobs in numbers, using FinMango's AI Economic Signal. A personal money plan for a non-linear career.

Weeks 3–6Build with AI

Teams of three pick a local financial barrier and build a tool, guide, data story or campaign. Weekly peer review against a rubric. AI does the grunt work; they do the judgment.

Weeks 7–8Ship and show

Published on finmango.org under their names. Five-minute Demo Day video scored by employer judges. Credential, feedback, introductions.

What participants leave with

  • A published project on finmango.org with a permanent link and a written build story.
  • A credential that verifies itself, issued only to people who shipped, linked to the work.
  • Demo Day scored by hiring managers, with written feedback from every judge. Prizes for the top three teams when sponsored.
  • Introductions to employer partners and priority for FinMango's Global Ambassador program.

No stipend or prizes are offered until a sponsor commits them. Tier 1 turns them on for one lean cohort: $100 per person when interviews are done, $200 when the project is published, never for enrolling. Tier 2 adds a half-time program lead, an instructor, an employer mentor per team, a live Demo Day and a published evaluation. Tier 3 adds a second cohort and the five-day classroom edition taught alongside Millionaire Mindset.

How we measure it all published

  • Skills: pre- and post-program assessment of AI fluency, financial planning and data reading.
  • Output: share of participants who ship a project; projects used by real people.
  • Outcomes: six-month follow-up on employment, education and earnings.
  • Voice: every participant review published unedited, as with our 16,000+ classroom reviews.
