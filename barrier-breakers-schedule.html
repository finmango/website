Schedule

Time Activity Details
8:00 - 9:00 AM Check-In & Poster Setup Breakfast snacks provided. Teams check in, get their poster number and section, and set up. Judges are briefed.
9:00 - 10:30 AM Poster Round Every team is judged. A panel of three judges visits each poster in its section: 60-second pitch, 2 minutes of Q&A, then scoring. Stay at your poster until your panel has been by.
10:30 - 10:50 AM Open Gallery & Judge Huddles Students, teachers, and guests browse the posters. Each judging panel confirms its section's finalist and runner-up.
10:50 AM Finalists Announced Five finalists (one per section) and five honorable mentions announced in the main room.
11:00 AM - 12:00 PM Lunch & Knowledge Track Lunch provided. Optional financial literacy quiz on your phone. Finalists get this hour to set up slides.
12:00 - 12:45 PM Demo Day Five finalists present to the Demo Day panel (the morning judges who stay) and the whole room. 5-minute pitch + 2-minute Q&A each.
12:45 - 1:15 PM Awards Demo Day places 1st through 5th, honorable mentions, and Knowledge Track winners.
1:15 - 2:00 PM Optional Tours Campus tours for those who can stay. Schools needing to leave may depart after awards.
2:00 PM Event Concludes All activities end by 2:00 PM.

What to Bring

  • Every team: your poster, plus a laptop or prototype if you have one
  • Every team: a rehearsed 60-second pitch. Judges keep time, and the stop is hard.
  • Just in case: a slide deck. Any team could be named a finalist, and slides are optional but recommended for Demo Day.
  • All attendees: your phone (for the Knowledge Track quiz)

Important Notes

  • Timing: with around 100 teams the poster round runs the full 90 minutes. With fewer, it ends early and the open gallery runs longer.
  • Food provided: breakfast snacks and lunch included for all attendees
  • Parking: free parking in designated lots (details sent before the event)
  • Dress code: business casual recommended for presenters
  • Guests welcome: family members, teachers, and non-competing students can attend
