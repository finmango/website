Barrier Breakers 2027

MONDAY, MARCH 8, 2027 · HOST CAMPUS TO BE ANNOUNCED

Schedule

Time Activity Details
8:30 - 9:15 AM Check-In & Setup Breakfast snacks provided. Teams check in, get their table number and section, and put up their poster and whatever else they brought to show. Judges are briefed.
9:15 - 9:20 AM Welcome A short welcome and the rules for the morning.
9:20 - 10:50 AM Poster Gallery Every team is judged. The room is split into ten sections, and a panel of two judges visits each table in its section: 60-second pitch, a few minutes of Q&A, then scoring. Stay at your table until your panel has been by.
10:50 - 11:15 AM Open Gallery & Judge Huddles Students, teachers, and guests browse the gallery. Each panel names its section's top team, and the five highest-scoring of those ten become the finalists.
11:15 - 11:25 AM Finalists Announced Five finalists and five honorable mentions announced in the main room. Finalists head to the stage.
11:25 AM - 12:05 PM Demo Day Five finalists present to the whole room and a separate Demo Day panel of five judges. 3-minute pitch + 2-minute Q&A each.
12:05 - 12:35 PM Lunch & Knowledge Track Lunch provided. Optional financial literacy quiz on your phone. The Demo Day judges rank the finalists.
12:35 - 1:15 PM Awards Demo Day places 1st through 5th, honorable mentions, and Knowledge Track winners.
1:15 PM Event Concludes The program ends at 1:15 PM and schools may depart. Optional campus tours follow for students and families who came on their own.

What to Bring

  • Every team: a poster for your table. Hand-made is fine. Bring anything else that makes your idea real too: a prototype, a 3D print, an app on a laptop.
  • Every team: a rehearsed 60-second pitch. Judges keep time, and the stop is hard.
  • Every team: a three-minute presentation, ready to go. Finalists are named at 11:15 and present at 11:25, and nobody knows in advance. Bring a short slide deck: three slides are enough, one for each judging question. Add the link when you register so it is ready on the day.
  • All attendees: your phone (for the Knowledge Track quiz)

Important Notes

  • Timing: with around 100 teams each panel sees about ten tables, so judges have several minutes with every team. With fewer teams the Poster Gallery ends early and the open gallery runs longer.
  • Food provided: breakfast snacks and lunch included for all attendees
  • Parking: free parking in designated lots (details sent before the event)
  • Dress code: business casual recommended for presenters
  • Guests welcome: family members, teachers, and non-competing students can attend
