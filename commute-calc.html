Why the far place costs more than its rent

"Drive till you qualify" is the standard advice for a first home. Here is what it leaves out.

Wear and tearEvery mile costs about 67 cents in gas, maintenance and depreciation at the IRS rate. A 60-mile round trip, five days a week, is over $10,000 a year in vehicle costs.
TimeTwo hours a day commuting is about 500 hours a year. That is 20 full days spent in a car, unpaid.
One breakdownWhen the job depends on the car, a breakdown means lost wages or a lost job. Closer in, transit, biking or walking give you another way to get there.
