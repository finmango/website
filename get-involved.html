Get involved

The affordability crisis won't fix itself. So we're fixing it.

FinMango is students, volunteers and researchers chipping away at it together — and it's working. Two ways in. Both start today.

A semester Become an ambassador Lead the work on your campus, backed by training, a global cohort, and our research. 2–5 hrs / week Volunteer with us Lend a few hours a week to the work. Remote, flexible, worldwide.
2–5 hours a week

Volunteer with us.

You don't need a finance background — you need a few hours and something you're already good at. Our volunteers build the tools, run the research, and get this work in front of the people who need it most.

  • Design
  • Research
  • Writing
  • Data analysis
  • Outreach
Apply to volunteer Questions first? Email us

Not sure you have the time? Apply anyway and say so — a couple of hours a week is enough to start.

  1. Apply Five minutes. The form asks what you're good at and how much time you have.
  2. We match you We read every application and reply with where you'd fit — a project, a team, a first task.
  3. You start Remote, on your own schedule, with real work from day one. Scale up or step back as life changes.
