Millionaire Mindset.

A five-day class that takes a student from never having invested to opening their first account. We teach it in person, free to the school and the student, and have reached more than 100,000 students so far.

What the five days cover.

Grades 9 to 12 and college. Students learn how compound interest works, how to read a fund, how to judge it against the market, and the mistakes that quietly cost people decades of return. Here is three minutes inside a real classroom.

  • Compound interestHow small, steady contributions grow into large sums, and why starting early matters more than starting big.
  • Reading a fundExpense ratios, returns and holdings, well enough to tell a great fund from an expensive one.
  • Judging it against the marketComparing a fund with the S&P 500 and other indexes to see what its performance is really worth.
  • The common mistakesMarket timing, high fees and panic selling, and the habits that avoid them.
  • Opening an accountChoosing a brokerage and opening the right account, step by step, until it is done.
  • Tax-free investingUsing a Roth IRA and other tax-advantaged accounts to keep more of what the growth earns.

We come in and teach all five days at no cost to your school. Or take the slides above and teach it yourself. Email us to book a week.

The man who teaches the week.

Bob Gillingham has taught high school students since 1997, first business basics through Junior Achievement and, since 2003, how to invest. With FinMango he teaches about 8,000 students a year across 27 high schools and colleges in five states. Before teaching, he owned an auto dealership.
