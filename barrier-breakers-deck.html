How teams are scored

Three questions

01

Do they get the problem?

Who it costs, how much, and why it’s stuck.

02

Would it actually work?

Something on the table, and a way it keeps running.

03

Could they explain it?

Clear in 60 seconds. Honest under questions.

Each scored 1 to 5 by every judge. The top team in each section goes to Demo Day.
