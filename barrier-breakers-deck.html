Why we run it

37% of American adults could not cover a $400 emergency with cash.

Many of our students see that at home. We ask them to pick one cost that holds families back, such as transportation, housing or healthcare, and build something that lowers it.

Source: Federal Reserve, Economic Well-Being of U.S. Households (SHED), fielded October 2025.

What students do

Students do the work of a founder.

1

They find a problem worth solving.

Teams choose a cost and learn who it hurts and what it costs them.

2

They build an answer.

An app, a product, a service or a program, with a plan to keep it running.

3

They pitch it.

Sixty seconds at their table, then questions from judges. Five teams present to the whole room.

How teams are scored

Judges ask three questions.

1

Do they get the problem?

Who it costs, how much, and why it is stuck.

2

Would it actually work?

Something on the table, and a way it keeps running.

3

Could they explain it?

Clear in 60 seconds. Honest under questions.

Both judges score each question from 1 to 5.

Who it reaches

FinMango teaches in more than 25 high schools around Akron, Canton and Cleveland. About 8,000 students hear about Barrier Breakers in class each year.

About 250 of them came to compete in 2026. For 2027 we are inviting more schools.
