Free tools for real money decisions.

Calculators, simulators and guides built on our own research. No sign-up. Open one and start.

Everyday money Big purchases Saving and retirement Family Help right now Small business Lessons and games

Everyday money

What you take home, and where it goes.

  • Paycheck CalculatorWhat actually lands in your account after federal and state tax, Social Security, Medicare and your own deductions.
  • Mexico Paycheck CalculatorYour take-home pay in Mexico after ISR and IMSS. In Spanish.
  • Company Benefits GuideHow to read a benefits package: 401(k), health insurance, HSA and FSA, stock options, time off, and what is worth negotiating.
  • What's Money CalculatorAdd up the small habitual spending and see what the same money would be worth invested.
  • The Minimum Payment TrapHow long paying only the minimum keeps you paying, and what one fixed payment of your own changes.
  • Student Loan SimulatorWhat paying a little extra each month does to your payoff date and your interest.

Big purchases

A home or a car, counted all the way through.

  • Housing CalculatorWhat a home would cost you each month, and how long the down payment would take at your pace.
  • Rent vs. Buy CalculatorPut in a home price and a comparable rent. We count the costs the mortgage payment leaves out and say which leaves you ahead.
  • New vs. Used Car CalculatorWhat each car costs over five years: payments, fuel, insurance, upkeep and the value it loses.

Saving and retirement

What the years do with the money you put away.

  • Compound Interest CalculatorPut in what you have and what you can add each month, and see what the years do with it.
  • Rule of 72 CalculatorDivide 72 by your return and you get the years it takes your money to double. Try it with your numbers.
  • Roth IRA CalculatorWhat a Roth IRA would be worth at 65 if you start now, none of it taxed.
  • Battle of the IRAsTraditional, Roth or a plain brokerage account. See which leaves you the most after tax.
  • FIRE CalculatorHow many years until your investments could cover your spending. Your savings rate decides it.
  • Portfolio SimulatorMix stocks and bonds, pick a span of years, and see roughly what it would grow into.
  • Inflation Time MachineWhat today's money buys in ten or thirty years, and what a dollar from the past was worth.

Family

Planning for the people who depend on you.

  • Family Financial PlanningPut in both salaries, time out of work, childcare, taxes and retirement savings, and see where a two-income family lands after 15 years.
  • Food Desert AnalyzerHow far the nearest full grocery store is from where you live, and what that does to a food budget.

Help right now

When something has gone wrong.

  • Food Assistance CalculatorA rough idea in three steps of whether SNAP is likely for your household, and where to apply.
  • Disaster DeskAfter a disaster, the aid you can get: FEMA, housing, food, medical and utility help, in three steps.

Small business

Starting one, and running it.

  • Startup CalculatorWhat it costs to open the doors, keep them open each month, and last until the business pays for itself.
  • Small Business Digital GuideHow to take a small business online, step by step: accounts, a website, ads, social media and analytics.

Lessons and games

Short lessons, and games that teach by playing.

  • Mango StoriesEleven short lessons told as stories: budgeting, credit, investing, student aid, buying a home, starting a business and more. Ten to thirty minutes each.
  • Table Banking GameRun a year inside a community savings group: chama, tanda, susu, stokvel. Vote on loans, weather an emergency, share out the box. Five languages, your local currency.
  • Sports Betting SimulatorPlace bets at the odds a real sportsbook posts and watch what a session does to your balance, your time and your sleep.

Missing something? Tell us what would help. Every tool here started as someone's question.
