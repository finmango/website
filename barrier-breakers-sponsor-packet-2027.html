Reach 8,000 students. Put the top 250 in one room.

Barrier Breakers is FinMango's one-day entrepreneurship competition for Northeast Ohio high schoolers, heading into its fourth year. In 2026 we promoted it to roughly 8,000 students in 25+ high schools across the Akron, Canton, and Cleveland areas through our in-person classroom programming over the academic year. About 250 of the top students from those classrooms showed up on March 20 to pitch real solutions to the costs that hold families back. Sponsors fund the prize pool and the day, and they meet those students face to face as judges. Levels and a commitment form are on pages 3 and 4.

How the pipeline works

In the classroom. FinMango teaches in-person financial education in 25+ high schools across the Akron, Canton, and Cleveland areas, reaching about 8,000 students over the academic year. That is where students first hear about Barrier Breakers.

The build. Teams of one to three pick a real barrier, such as transportation, housing, healthcare, or financial literacy, and build a solution to bring to the event.

Event day. Sixty-second pitches to a three-judge panel in the morning. Five finalists present to the whole room at Demo Day in the afternoon, and the cash goes to the teams.

What a sponsor gets on the day

Our 2026 presenting sponsor was visible to every student in the building, from check-in through the awards. Every level puts your name in front of the students, and every level above Silver puts your people on the judging panel.

  • Logo on the site, the printed program, and, at higher levels, every banner and student handout
  • Your employees judge: sixty-second pitches at the tables, Demo Day for the top levels
  • A table and banner on the gallery floor for the presenting sponsor
  • Sponsor representatives present awards alongside the judging panel

Where the money went in 2026

Barrier Breakers 2026 raised $30,000 in total: $25,000 from the presenting sponsor and $5,000 from two Silver sponsors. More than $20,000 went out as awards. The rest paid for the day itself: breakfast and lunch for 250 students plus their teachers and judges, materials, and the space.

In 2026 a sizable share of the awards went to scholarships through the host university rather than to the students in the room. Scholarships will not come out of the sponsorship budget in 2027. Sponsor dollars go to the prizes and the event itself, managed directly by FinMango.

Sponsoring Barrier Breakers 2027

The day keeps the same rhythm, a morning of pitches at the tables and Demo Day in the afternoon, with a simpler format for 2027: one pipeline for every team, no tracks to declare, one rubric for both rounds, and cash straight to the student teams. With no scholarship component, more of each sponsor dollar reaches the students in the room.

What sponsorship funds

The prize pool. Cash to the five Demo Day teams, the largest share of the $20,000+ awarded in 2026.

The day. Breakfast, lunch, and materials for 250 students plus their teachers and judges.

Operations and contingency. Venue, AV, printing, insurance, and a reserve for the unexpected costs of an event this size.

FinMango is all-volunteer with no paid staff, so the full sponsorship is invested in the event: prizes first, then the day.
