Barrier Breakers · Northeast Ohio's high school entrepreneurship competition · fourth year

Budgeting advice doesn't fix a $400 ER bill or rent that eats half a paycheck. A student who has lived those costs might. Barrier Breakers gives Northeast Ohio high schoolers a real barrier to financial health, a few months to build something that breaks it, and a room full of local business leaders to pitch it to. The best ventures leave with cash.

8,000Students FinMango teaches in person each year across 25+ Akron, Canton, and Cleveland-area high schools. That is where teams come from.
250Students in the room on March 20, 2026: 104 teams, judged by 12 volunteer professionals in one day.
$20K+Awarded in 2026 to winning teams and their schools. Sponsors fund it. FinMango is all-volunteer, so nearly every dollar reaches students.
01 · FIND THE COSTPick a barrier

Healthcare, housing, transportation, financial literacy, or one the team has noticed. Research who it hurts and what it costs them.

02 · BUILDMake it real

An app, a product, a service, a policy. Work out who pays and who runs it. Bring a prototype, not a slide about one.

03 · PITCHSixty seconds, then questions

Three judges at every table. Five finalists present to the whole room at Demo Day. Cash goes straight to the teams.

What the judges saw

  • A medical bill analyzer that reads a bill, flags errors, and finds assistance. Solves a real problem that costs Americans billions, and they've built it.
  • A paycheck scanner that turns a pay stub into a budget. I love the paycheck scanning.
  • Low-cost rides to medical appointments, built with hospitals and insurers in mind.
  • A tax-credit tool that automates an existing, rarely used IRS code. No new laws needed.
  • Surplus food to hungry families, modeled on programs that work abroad. Already developed beyond the idea stage.
